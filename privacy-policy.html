Privacy Policy

Your privacy is critically important to us. This policy outlines how KhabarX collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website.

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

Zero Data Selling

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or email address to third parties.

Smart Local Storage

We use browser storage solely to remember your preferred reading language (Roman, Urdu, or English).

Safe Browsing

Our platform is built with modern security standards to ensure a safe reading experience on every device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to KhabarX. By accessing and using our website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of our website.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. When you browse our news articles, we do not require you to create an account. However, we may collect limited information in the following ways:

  • Newsletter Subscriptions: If you voluntarily subscribe to our Daily Updates in the footer, we collect your email address to send you news alerts.
  • Log & Device Data: Like most standard websites, our servers may automatically record non-personal technical data such as your browser type, device screen size, and general region to optimize website performance.

3. Local Storage & Cookies

KhabarX uses browser Local Storage to enhance your user experience. Specifically, when you select a preferred language (Roman Urdu, Urdu, or English) from the top menu, that preference is saved locally on your device so you do not have to re-select it on every page. You can clear this data anytime through your browser settings.

4. How We Use Your Information

Any information collected is used strictly to:

  • Deliver accurate, real-time news in your chosen language.
  • Improve website speed, mobile responsiveness, and search accuracy.
  • Send daily news digests to users who have explicitly opted into our newsletter.

5. Third-Party Links & Advertising

Our articles may occasionally contain links to external websites or third-party media resources. Please note that we do not control the privacy practices of external sites. Additionally, if we display third-party advertisements (such as Google AdSense) in the future, those partners may use cookies to serve relevant ads based on your browsing interests.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data (such as your newsletter email) that you have shared with us. You may also unsubscribe from our emails at any time.

7. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to our editorial and compliance team through our Contact Us page.

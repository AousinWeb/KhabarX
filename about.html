OUR STORY

Why We Created KhabarX

In today's fast-paced digital age, social media is often flooded with unverified rumors and misleading headlines. At the same time, millions of readers prefer reading news either in easy-to-follow Roman Urdu, traditional Urdu, or global English.

To bridge this gap, we founded KhabarX—a clutter-free platform where a single click switches the entire story into your chosen language, delivering only accurate and meaningful journalism.

  • Unbiased, fact-based reporting without sensationalism
  • Instant multi-language smart search and category filtering
  • Comprehensive coverage across Pakistan, World, Sports, Tech, and Business
